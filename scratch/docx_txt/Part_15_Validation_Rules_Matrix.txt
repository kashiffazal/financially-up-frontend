Financially Up Individual Engagement SystemDeveloper SpecificationPart 15 - Validation Rules Matrix
Purpose
This document consolidates the validation requirements for the client engagement wizard. It is intended as a developer reference for implementing Ant Design form validation.
Validation Principles
Perform client-side validation using Ant Design Form rules.
Repeat all critical validation on the server.
Display clear error messages.
Hide validation for fields that are not currently visible.
Prevent submission until all mandatory validations pass.
