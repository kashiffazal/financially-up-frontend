Source Document: Financially Up Pty Ltd Individual Engagement - Latest Specification v2.1 (24 July 2026). This document summarizes and structures the specification for software implementation. 
1. Project Overview
The Individual Engagement System is an online onboarding portal used by Financially Up Pty Ltd. It allows individual clients and sole traders to engage Financially Up for taxation and related services. The portal collects client information, identity documents, authorities, consents and electronic signatures before an accountant reviews the engagement.
2. Objectives
Provide a guided multi-step engagement wizard.
Display only relevant questions using conditional logic.
Collect legally required authorities and declarations.
Support identity verification and document uploads.
Generate an Engagement Schedule automatically.
Keep the engagement in Pending Review until accepted by staff.
Provide a secure administration portal for internal review.

Manages configuration, document rules, fees, users and audit logs.
