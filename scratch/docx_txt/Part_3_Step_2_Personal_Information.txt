Financially Up Individual Engagement SystemDeveloper SpecificationPart 3 - Step 2: Personal Information
Step Objective
Collect the client's identity and contact information required to prepare the engagement. Many fields are conditionally displayed based on the selected services and user responses.
Business Rules
Only collect information reasonably required for the selected services.
TFN is only displayed when the selected service requires it.
Previous Name is displayed only when the client confirms they have used another name.
Country of Birth is mandatory.
About Yourself is limited to 500 characters.

Conditional Logic
If 'Have you used another name?' = Yes → Display Previous / Other Names.
If selected service requires TFN → Display TFN field.
If TFN alternative selected → Hide mandatory TFN input, display Explanation field and create accountant review flag.
Acceptance Criteria
Full Legal Name is mandatory.
Date of Birth is mandatory.
Country of Birth is mandatory.
Email must be valid.
Mobile number must be valid.
TFN only appears for applicable services.
About Yourself accepts a maximum of 500 characters.
