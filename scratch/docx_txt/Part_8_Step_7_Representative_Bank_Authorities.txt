Financially Up Individual Engagement SystemDeveloper SpecificationPart 8 - Step 7: Representative, Bank Details & Authorities
Step Objective
Collect representative information (where applicable), bank details when required, and the client authorities required to interact with the ATO, ABR and previous advisers.
Business Rules
Default assumption is the client is completing the form for themselves.
If a representative completes the form, both identities and authority must be verified.
Estate and deceased matters must not continue in this workflow and must be redirected to the appropriate engagement.
Bank details are only collected when required by the selected service.
ATO Authority is mandatory.
ABR Authority is mandatory only when ABN/GST services are selected.
Previous Adviser Authority is displayed only if the client had a previous accountant.

Conditional Logic
If 'Completing for Yourself' = No → Display representative fields and verify both client and representative.
If representative is Guardian, Attorney or Executor → Require authority document before submission.
If matter relates to a deceased estate → Stop workflow and redirect to Estate Engagement.
If bank details are required → Display bank fields and verify account ownership.
If ABN/GST service selected → Display ABR Authority.
If previous accountant exists → Display Previous Adviser Authority.
Internal Actions
Create manual review for representative engagements.
Verify authority documents before acceptance.
Mask bank account details after submission.
Re-verify changed bank details through a trusted communication channel.
Record ATO/ABR authority selections in the audit log.
Acceptance Criteria
Representative fields remain hidden unless required.
Authority documents are mandatory for representatives.
Estate engagements cannot continue in this wizard.
Bank details are only requested when applicable.
ATO Authority must be accepted before submission.
ABR Authority is required only for ABN/GST services.
