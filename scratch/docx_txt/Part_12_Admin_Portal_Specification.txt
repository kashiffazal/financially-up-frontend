Financially Up Individual Engagement SystemDeveloper SpecificationPart 12 - Admin Portal Specification
Purpose
This document specifies the internal administration portal used by Financially Up staff after a client submits an engagement. These screens are internal only and must never be visible in the client-facing portal.
User Roles
Role
Responsibilities
Administrator
Manage users, configuration, document rules, fees and system settings.
Accountant
Review engagements, request information, accept or decline engagements.
Compliance Officer
Review AML/CTF, sanctions and high-risk engagements.

Acceptance Criteria
Client cannot access admin screens.
Only authorised staff can change engagement status.
Every decision is audited.
High-risk engagements require escalation.
Accepted engagements generate an Engagement Acceptance Notice.
