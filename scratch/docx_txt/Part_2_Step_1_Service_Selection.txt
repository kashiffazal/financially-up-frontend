Financially Up Individual Engagement SystemDeveloper SpecificationPart 2 - Step 1: Service Selection
Purpose
This step determines the services requested by the client and drives the remaining wizard using conditional logic.
Business Requirements
Client must select at least one service.
Questions shown later depend on selected services.
If entity-related work is selected, a separate Entity Engagement must be created while allowing the individual engagement to continue.

Conditional Logic
If Entity Service = Yes
Continue the Individual Engagement wizard.
Automatically create a linked Business / Entity Engagement record.
Notify internal staff that a second engagement is required.
Do not ask company/trust fields inside this individual form.
If Entity Service = No or Unsure
Continue normal Individual Engagement flow.
Recommended Ant Design Model
{  services: [],  entityService: null}
Acceptance Criteria
User cannot continue without selecting at least one service.
Entity question is mandatory.
Entity fields are not collected in this form.
Selection is stored successfully.
Next step depends on selected services.
