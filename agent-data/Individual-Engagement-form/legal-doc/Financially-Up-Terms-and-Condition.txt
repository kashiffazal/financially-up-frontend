1. Parties and contract documents
These Terms form part of the engagement between Financially Up Pty Ltd ABN 84 659 717 263 (Financially Up, we, us or our) and the individual named in the Client Details and Service Form (you or your).
The engagement consists of the written Engagement Acceptance Notice, Engagement Schedule, accepted client form, these Terms, Privacy Collection Notice, Privacy Policy, TPB Client Information Statement, recorded authorities and consents, and any written variation accepted by both parties.

Submitting a form does not require Financially Up to accept the engagement. We may complete identity, capacity, authority, conflict, risk, sanctions, professional and AML/CTF checks before deciding whether to act.

3. Scope and Engagement Schedule
We will provide only the services expressly listed in the Engagement Schedule for the stated client and period. The Schedule records the deliverables, inclusions, exclusions, information deadline, expected timing, fee and special conditions.
Additional years, entities, transactions, representations, bookkeeping corrections, audit work, objections or advice are outside scope unless accepted in a written variation.
4. Excluded professional services
Unless expressly listed in the Engagement Schedule, Financially Up does not provide legal, financial product, investment, credit, mortgage, immigration, insolvency, valuation, audit, assurance or forensic services. You should obtain advice from an appropriately qualified and licensed professional where required.
A referral or introduction does not make Financially Up responsible for another provider’s advice or services.
5. BAS service scope
The Engagement Schedule must state whether Financially Up will prepare from client-maintained records, review GST coding, perform bank or payroll reconciliations, correct records, review prior periods, calculate fuel tax credits, or prepare and lodge only.
Where Financially Up does not maintain the underlying records, we may rely on the records supplied and will not independently verify every transaction or balance. Transaction coding, reconciliation, payroll review, prior-period corrections and bookkeeping reconstruction are excluded unless expressly included.
6. Client responsibilities
You must provide complete, truthful, accurate and timely information and documents, respond to reasonable requests, keep contact and authority details current and disclose matters relevant to the accepted service.
You must not request Financially Up or any person working for it to create, backdate, alter, conceal, omit or misrepresent information or to lodge material that is false, misleading, unsupported, unlawful or unethical.
Disclose all relevant income, deductions, assets, liabilities, investments, overseas interests, cryptocurrency, business activity and GST matters.
Maintain records required by law.
Review all documents before approval.
Notify us immediately of errors, changes, regulator correspondence and deadlines.
Cooperate with identity, sanctions, fraud prevention, acceptance and AML/CTF procedures.
