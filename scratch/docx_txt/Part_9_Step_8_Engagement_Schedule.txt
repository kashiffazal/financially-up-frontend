Financially Up Individual Engagement SystemDeveloper SpecificationPart 9 - Step 8: Engagement Schedule
Step Objective
Generate a client-specific Engagement Schedule summarising the exact scope of work. The schedule is automatically produced from the client's responses and must be reviewed before the client signs the engagement.
Business Rules
Engagement Schedule is generated automatically and is read-only for the client.
It must be displayed before the consent and signature step.
Submission is blocked until the client opens the Engagement Schedule.
The schedule is versioned and stored with the signed engagement.
A written Engagement Acceptance Notice is still required after internal review.

Generation Rules
Populate services from Step 1.
Populate client details from previous steps.
Calculate included/excluded work based on selected services.
Display configured fees and conditions.
Generate a PDF-ready summary.
Version every generated schedule.
Client Workflow
System generates Engagement Schedule.
Client reviews summary.
Client opens full Terms & Conditions.
Client proceeds to consent screen.
Internal Workflow
Accountant reviews generated schedule.
Amend scope if required.
Issue Engagement Acceptance Notice after approval.
Store final accepted version with audit trail.
Acceptance Criteria
Schedule is generated automatically.
Client cannot edit generated values.
Client must open the schedule before signing.
Version number is stored.
Final PDF matches accepted schedule.
