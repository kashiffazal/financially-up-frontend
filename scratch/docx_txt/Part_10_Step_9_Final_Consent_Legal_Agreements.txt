Financially Up Individual Engagement SystemDeveloper SpecificationPart 10 - Step 9: Final Consent & Legal Agreements
Step Objective
Before the client signs the engagement, all required legal documents must be opened and the required consents recorded separately. No checkbox may be pre-selected.
Business Rules
All required documents must be opened before the signature step is enabled.
Every consent is stored independently in the audit trail.
No checkbox or radio option may be pre-selected.
If biometric verification is not used, biometric consent must remain hidden.
Recording consent is optional and shown only when interviews are recorded.
Submission is blocked until all mandatory consents are accepted.

Client Declarations
I confirm the information supplied is true and complete.
I have disclosed all relevant income, deductions, GST matters and overseas matters.
I will notify Financially Up of changes or errors.
I remain responsible for reviewing documents before lodgement.
I understand Financially Up cannot guarantee taxation outcomes.
I will not request false or misleading information.
I understand my engagement remains Pending Review until accepted.
I consent to electronic transactions and electronic signature.
Blocking Conditions
Required documents not opened.
Mandatory consent missing.
Technology consent not answered.
Biometric consent missing when biometric verification is selected.
Audit Trail Requirements
Record document open timestamps.
Store consent wording and selections.
Store document version numbers.
Store date/time of acceptance.
Record IP/device information where implemented.
Link all consents to the final engagement.
Acceptance Criteria
Client cannot continue without mandatory consents.
No consent is pre-selected.
Conditional consents appear only when applicable.
Audit trail is generated successfully.
