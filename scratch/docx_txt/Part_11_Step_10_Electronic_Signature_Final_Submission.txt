Financially Up Individual Engagement SystemDeveloper SpecificationPart 11 - Step 10: Electronic Signature & Final Submission
Step Objective
Capture the client's legally binding electronic signature, record all required audit evidence, finalize the engagement package, and submit the engagement for internal review. After submission, the engagement status must remain 'Pending Review' until Financially Up issues a written Engagement Acceptance Notice.
Business Rules
Client must complete all previous steps before signing.
Signature is blocked until mandatory documents are opened and required consents are accepted.
Electronic signature must be linked to the exact document versions accepted.
Submission does not mean the engagement has been accepted.
Status after submission is 'Pending Review'.
Only authorised staff may change the engagement to Accepted, Conditional Accept, Request Information or Declined.

Verify all required consents.

Acceptance Criteria
Client cannot submit without completing all required steps.
Signature is captured successfully.
Audit trail is complete.
Status changes to Pending Review.
Engagement package is generated successfully.
