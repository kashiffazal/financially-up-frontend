Financially Up Individual Engagement SystemDeveloper SpecificationPart 18 - PDF Generation, Email Workflow & AI Development Guide
Purpose
This document complements Parts 1-17 and explains how the completed engagement should be converted into professional PDFs, emailed to clients and staff, and implemented as a new module within the existing Financially Up website and admin portal.
1. Recommended Architecture
Frontend: Next.js + Ant Design
Backend: Node.js + Express/Nest (existing API)
MySQL + Sequelize
Puppeteer for PDF generation
Nodemailer (or SMTP provider) for email delivery
Background queue (BullMQ + Redis) for PDF/email jobs (recommended)

3. Email Workflow
Client submits engagement.
Save all data.
Generate Client PDF.
Generate Admin PDF.
Email Client PDF to the client.
Email Admin PDF to configured staff mailbox.
Set status to Pending Review.
When accepted, generate Acceptance PDF and email the client.
