Financially Up Individual Engagement SystemDeveloper SpecificationPart 5 - Step 4: Income & Tax Profile
Step Objective
Collect the client's income sources, previous accountant information and any ATO issues. The answers determine document requests, review flags and follow-up workflow.
Business Rules
At least one income/activity should be selected.
Previous accountant questions appear only when applicable.
ATO debt, audits or unresolved correspondence require additional information and document upload.
Disputes with a previous accountant create an internal review flag.
ATO issues create a priority review task.

Conditional Logic
If Previous Accountant = Yes → Display Previous Firm, Reason for Change and Authorisation fields.
If Reason for Change indicates dispute or involuntary disengagement → Create Internal Review Flag.
If ATO Issues = Yes or Unsure → Display explanation, notice date, due date and upload fields.
If ATO Issues = Yes or Unsure → Create Priority Review Task for accountant.
Internal System Actions
Flag disputed previous accountant relationships.
Create priority review for ATO matters.
Link uploaded ATO documents to engagement.
Include selected income types in dynamic document request engine.
Acceptance Criteria
At least one income source is selected.
Conditional fields remain hidden until triggered.
ATO uploads are mandatory when displayed.
Review flags are generated correctly.
Submitted values are stored successfully.
